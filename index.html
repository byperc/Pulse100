About the data and rankings

The included demonstration records use illustrative peak and weeks values. Genre labels supplied through enrichment files are user-provided and should be verified. Imported records are displayed as provided; confirm their provenance and usage rights before public publication. For imported weekly charts, custom score is the sum of (101 − weekly rank) over all chart appearances; for manually imported CSVs, it is (101 − peak) × weeks. Neither is an official chart methodology. YouTube Music buttons open a search for the song and artist, not a guaranteed exact recording. Historical Hot 100 import uses a third-party community archive, not an official Billboard feed. Publication and redistribution rights must be reviewed before public launch. Genre information is not supplied by this source, so imported songs are marked Unclassified until enriched. Artist-level genres are estimates and do not prove a specific recording's genre. A song’s year is its first chart appearance, not necessarily its release year.
